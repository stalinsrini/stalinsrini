# Ayadoure Srinivasane (Stalin)

### Researcher in International Political Economy | Climate Finance | Disaster Risk Reduction

I am a doctoral researcher at the Centre for Indo-Pacific Studies, School of International Studies, Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU), India.

My doctoral research examines the political economy of multilateral development finance, disaster risk reduction, climate governance, and Southeast Asia, with a particular focus on Indonesia and ASEAN.

## Doctoral Research

**Political Economy of Policy-Based Lending for Disaster Risk Reduction in Indonesia, 2005–2025**

My doctoral research compares the World Bank and the Asian Development Bank's initiatives, examining policy-based lending, development policy alignment, and disaster risk governance in Indonesia.
